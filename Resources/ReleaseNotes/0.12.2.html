Savenest 0.12.2 — Glass appearance and easier discovery

  • Midnight and Pearl glass appearances across the launcher, reading workspace, and native controls, with solid surfaces for reduced transparency or increased contrast.
  • Settings and Shortcuts are visible in the launcher footer. Command-/ opens a guide with launcher keys and your configured global shortcuts.
  • Actions now includes search scopes, capture, and library tools, even when a search has no results. Search saved links appears first in its Search group.
  • Keeps the compact search layout, borderless search field, and inset dropdown arrow.
  • Includes the launcher dismissal fix after saving a link with a note.

This remains a preview for macOS 14 or later. Full installed-app migration, Intel runtime, and cloud-synced concurrent-edit acceptance remain pending.
