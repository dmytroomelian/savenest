What’s new in Savenest 0.9.7

  • Search your whole collection—including archived links—or narrow results to Library, Favorites, Notes, or Archive with tag, domain, and note filters.
  • Close source tabs after adding or changing notes and tags, using the same setting as saving links.
  • Close duplicate and empty tabs with Command-Shift-D.
  • Clean up tabs from selected websites, with a choice to review each cleanup or close matches right away.
  • Use Command-Option-T for the memorable tab-saving shortcut preset.
