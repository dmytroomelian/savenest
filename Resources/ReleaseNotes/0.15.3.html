Savenest 0.15.3

  • Close Reader with Command-W, Escape, or the window close button. Choose whether to keep the original browser tab open or archive the article and close its tab. Pending notes are saved before closing.
  • Remember either closing choice. Press Shift-Escape to change it or restore the question on every close.
  • Command-Shift-W saves notes, archives the article, and closes its original browser tab directly. Changed or ambiguous tabs stay open with an explanation.
  • Links in the original webpage, including links that open a new tab, offer an Open link action. The action stays visible beside the webpage without replacing your article or note.
