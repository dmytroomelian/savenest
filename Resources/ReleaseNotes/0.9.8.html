What's new

  • Savenest can now suggest up to three existing tags from page titles, website history, and patterns in your saved links.
  • Auto-tagging works with Quick Save and Save with a note, stays entirely on your Mac, and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Save with tags is now part of Save with a note, giving every captured link one consistent editor.
  • Press Tab to accept the first matching tag suggestion. Suggested casing follows how you type.
  • Sort the Tags library alphabetically or by number of links.
  • Settings are now split into focused General, Capture, and Library sections.
