Savenest 0.11.1 — Free Mac preview

  • Local saving, notes, tags, search, import, and export are free. No account or subscription is required.
  • Press ⌘O to search saved links, open tabs, and recent browser history in one launcher.
  • Anonymous usage counts can be turned off in Settings or the first-use notice.
  • Cloud backup and sync are coming later. Your library stays on your Mac.

When this preview is offered in native Savenest 0.10.0, choose Check for Updates… and follow the installer to switch to the new app. Export a local backup before upgrading. macOS may ask you to grant browser access again.

This remains a preview. Full installed-app checks for library and account preservation, offline use, import, and export are still pending. Future Electron updates use Savenest → Check & Download Updates.
