Savenest 0.12.3 — Working update checks in Settings

  • Check for updates directly from Settings, with progress, retry, and install controls.
  • Manual checks remain available after errors and when your app is already up to date. Automatic checks are not required.
  • Install an update with Settings open. Unfinished notes, account input, and other editing dialogs still protect against an unexpected restart.
  • Recover the launcher if installation cannot start, keeping the native helper and your work available.

This remains a preview for macOS 14 or later. Full installed-app migration, Intel runtime, and cloud-synced concurrent-edit acceptance remain pending.
